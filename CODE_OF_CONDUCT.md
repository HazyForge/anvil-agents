# Code Of Conduct

Project spaces should remain professional, respectful, and focused on building
reliable software. Participants must not harass, threaten, discriminate
against, or publish private information about others. Technical disagreement
is expected; personal attacks and sustained disruption are not.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report
conduct concerns privately to the repository owners. Reports will be handled
with appropriate confidentiality and conflicts of interest will be avoided.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, release
channels, and public representation of the project.
